Walkie-talkie for your Pebble – with text. Pick a friend, press SELECT, speak: your words arrive as a text message on their watch. End-to-end encrypted, open source, and you can run your own server.

WatchieTalkie2 continues the original Watchie-Talkie app, which has been offline for years. It is a free hobby project: a just-for-fun chat with your friends, right from your wrist.

• Speech to text via Pebble dictation (watches with microphone)
• Quick replies you define yourself (also for watches without microphone)
• Usernames, invitations, blocking
• Direct chats and groups (up to 20 people)
• History: last 50 messages per chat, kept for 30 days
• End-to-end encryption (NaCl): the server operator cannot read your messages
• Notifications even when the app is closed (timeline)
• Every Pebble: Pebble/Steel, Time, Time Steel, Time Round, Pebble 2, Time 2, Pebble 2 Duo, Round 2
• Open source (MIT) – host your own server with Node.js, no database needed

Getting started: open the app settings in the Pebble app, choose a username and invite friends by their username. On the watch: SELECT = speak, long SELECT = quick reply.

Beta: use at your own risk, no guarantee of availability or delivery. Not for emergencies.

Source code & self-hosting: https://github.com/sgitaize/Pebble-WatchieTalkie
