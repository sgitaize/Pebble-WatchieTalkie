1.10: Voice test for yourself on Pebble Time 2 and Pebble 2 Duo: hold SELECT in the chat list → "Voice test" (or use the WatchieTalkie system chat). Record a few words and the watch plays them straight back – end-to-end encrypted to yourself and decrypted again, so microphone, encryption and speaker are tested without sending anything to anyone. Text sent into the system chat now comes back as an echo instead of "read-only".
1.9: Voice messages on Pebble Time 2 and Pebble 2 Duo: hold SELECT in a chat → "Voice message", talk up to 30 seconds, SELECT sends. Play them through the watch speaker (hold SELECT → "Play latest" or "Voice messages …"). The option only appears when both sides have a Pebble with microphone and speaker – the app checks this automatically. End-to-end encrypted like every message. Other Pebble models keep dictation as before.
1.8: Pebble Time 2, Pebble 2 Duo and Round 2: emoji now come from PebbleOS itself – every emoji in messages, chat names and previews is shown (in color from PebbleOS 4.39 on) instead of a text smiley or "(emoji)", the emoji picker has 25 emoji, and messages with only 1-3 emoji are shown larger. Older Pebble models keep every feature and the emoji pictures from 1.6.
1.7: After dictating, the watch shows the full recognized text before sending – scroll with UP/DOWN, SELECT sends, BACK discards (speech recognition can occasionally invent whole sentences from silence or background noise). Every message now shows its date and time in small print, and dictated messages are marked "dictated".
1.6: Real emoji on the watch: the emoji picker shows pictures, and messages that consist only of emoji (up to 3) appear as pictures in the chat. Hold SELECT in a chat: Emoji, Delete and Pause push are now at the top, quick replies below (without a microphone, SELECT still jumps straight to the first quick reply). Loading: if the phone is not connected, the watch says so right away and continues loading automatically once the connection is back; status texts "Connecting to phone …" and "Loading chats …" instead of a plain "Loading".
1.5.1: The text size setting now also applies to the chat list and the menus (quick replies, emoji, invitations, push pause).
1.5: Pause phone notifications (ntfy/Telegram/Pushover) from the watch: hold SELECT in the chat list or a chat → "Pause push" (duration set in the settings). New setting: no further notification within X minutes after one. The radio beep switch moved into the same menu (hold SELECT in the chat list). New in the settings under "Display on the watch": text size (small, large bold, extra large bold), background and text color with presets and a live preview, and "keep the backlight on while the app is open". Better default look: black text on white, your own messages in light gray instead of pink. Fix: the selection in the chat list no longer jumps to the top when the list refreshes.
1.4: Emoji picker (hold SELECT in a chat → Emoji …) – sends real emoji, shown as text smileys on the watch. Delete your last message in a chat (hold SELECT → Delete my last message) – it disappears for everyone. Group owners can remove members in the settings. If the server is down or busy, the watch now shows an error and retries automatically instead of staying on "Loading".
1.3: Hold SELECT in the chat list to mute/unmute the radio beep (crossed speaker in the header). No beep and no vibration during Quiet Time. Optional phone notifications when the app is closed via ntfy, Telegram or Pushover (set up in the settings).
