                Phone notification (optional)

                Why? A Pebble watch app cannot listen for messages while it is closed, and the new Pebble app (Core) does not deliver timeline notifications from servers. So the only way to get a buzz while WatchieTalkie2 is closed is a normal phone notification, which the Pebble app forwards to your watch. Pick one of these apps for that. Totally optional: without it, new messages still arrive whenever the app is open on the watch. The notification only says "New message: <sender>", never the message itself (that stays end-to-end encrypted).
